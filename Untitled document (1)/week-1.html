Week 1 – 5S Activity and Portfolio Development

The first week of the internship started with an introduction to workplace organization through a practical 5S activity, which was carried out during the first three days. The main objective of this activity was to understand how a clean, organized, and properly labeled workspace improves productivity, minimizes errors, and promotes safety. It also demonstrated how the 5S methodology is applied in industrial and engineering environments to create an efficient workplace.

I was a member of the Battery Team, where our responsibility was to organize the different types of cables available in the workspace. We carefully sorted the cables according to their type, size, and purpose before placing them into properly labeled storage boxes. During the first day, we identified each cable, grouped similar ones together, and arranged them systematically so they could be located easily whenever required. This task required careful observation because incorrect labeling or improper arrangement could create confusion in future operations.

On the second day, after completing most of the cable classification work, I continued assisting my team by finishing the remaining organizational tasks. We verified the labels, improved the arrangement of the cables, and ensured that every storage box followed the same organizational standard. Working together throughout this process highlighted the importance of teamwork and effective communication. Even a simple task such as organizing cables becomes more efficient when responsibilities are shared and everyone follows a common approach.

The third day focused on completing the final stage of the 5S activity. All the labeled cable boxes were arranged neatly inside the storage cabinet in a logical sequence, making them easy to access whenever needed. We ensured that the storage area remained clean, organized, and convenient for future use. This stage clearly demonstrated the practical implementation of the five principles of 5S—Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardize, and Sustain—and showed how proper organization contributes to an efficient and productive workplace.

During the final two days of the week, our attention shifted from workplace organization to portfolio development. We began creating our personal portfolios by compiling our educational qualifications, technical skills, projects, achievements, certifications, and career goals into a professional format. The activity encouraged us to think carefully about how to present ourselves effectively, identify our strengths, and organize our accomplishments in a way that would leave a positive impression on recruiters and industry professionals. It also emphasized the importance of personal branding alongside technical knowledge.

Overall, Week 1 provided a balanced learning experience by combining practical workplace discipline with professional development. The 5S activity strengthened my understanding of organization, teamwork, and attention to detail, while the portfolio development sessions improved my ability to present my skills and experiences professionally. Together, these activities established a solid foundation for the remaining weeks of the internship and prepared me for both technical and career-oriented growth.
