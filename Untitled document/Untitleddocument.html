Week 0 – Orientation & Introduction

The ProtoSem journey began in Week 0 with an orientation session that introduced the overall structure and expectations of the semester. The session explained how the program would be conducted, including the weekly schedule of activities, assignments, progress tracking, and documentation that would continue until Week 20. It provided a clear understanding of the program's workflow and established the expectations for consistency, participation, and commitment throughout the semester.

One of the key activities during the orientation was completing the 16Personalities assessment. My result identified me as a Campaigner (ENFP-T), a personality type recognized for being creative, enthusiastic, and people-focused, with a strong interest in discovering new ideas and approaching challenges from unique perspectives. Campaigners are often characterized as energetic problem-solvers who enjoy innovation, adaptability, and flexibility rather than following strictly structured methods. They are naturally curious and motivated to explore opportunities for learning and growth. Reviewing the results encouraged self-reflection, and I found that many of the characteristics aligned with my approach to engineering projects, where I prefer experimentation, collaboration, and adaptable thinking instead of relying solely on predefined procedures.

The week concluded with a self-introduction session involving fellow participants and mentors. This provided an opportunity for everyone to introduce themselves, discuss their backgrounds, interests, and objectives for the semester. During my introduction, I shared my academic background in Mechatronics Engineering, my passion for robotics, automation, and artificial intelligence, and my expectations from the ProtoSem program. It also served as an excellent opportunity to meet the peers and mentors I would be working alongside throughout the semester, helping establish the foundation for effective teamwork and collaboration in the weeks ahead.
