Protosem Immersion Program

Week 0 — Orientation & Introduction

The Protosem officially kicked off in Week 0 with an orientation session that laid the groundwork for the entire semester. The session explained the weekly rhythm of tasks and assignments, as well as how progress would be tracked and documented through Week 20.

16 Personalities Activity

A key part of the orientation was a self-assessment exercise using the 16Personalities test. My result was the Campaigner (ENFP-T), a personality type known for imagination, energy, curiosity, and a people-oriented approach to exploring new ideas.

Reflection

The week closed with self-introductions to the batch and mentors. I shared my Mechatronics Engineering background, interest in robotics, automation, and AI, and my goals for the Protosem experience. It was an important first step toward building a collaborative team for the semester ahead.
