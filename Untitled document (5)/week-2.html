Week 2 – Learning, Thinking & Building

Week 2 was mainly about improving my thinking skills and getting started with programming and application development. We had a Design Thinking session conducted by Meera Ma’am, where I learned how to approach a problem before directly trying to find a solution. The session helped me understand the importance of looking at a problem from different perspectives and coming up with different ideas.

Along with Design Thinking, I attended sessions on Logical Thinking and Algorithms. We were given different activities to improve our way of thinking and problem-solving. We worked on the activities as a team and stuck our answers and ideas on the wall. Seeing the different approaches taken by others helped me understand that the same problem can have different solutions.

I also started learning the basic fundamentals of Python. I was introduced to the basic concepts of programming and started understanding how logic can be converted into code. This was useful because the logical thinking activities helped me understand the programming concepts better.

MIT App Inventor – Vehicle Expense Tracker

During the week, my team also worked on a mobile application using MIT App Inventor. We developed an app called Vehicle Expense Tracker. The idea was to create an application that could be used to record and manage vehicle-related expenses.

We worked on the different parts of the application and learned how to use the components and block-based programming in MIT App Inventor. Working on the app gave me a basic understanding of how a mobile application is planned, designed and developed.

Scratch – Pac-Man Game

We also had a team activity using Scratch, where our team developed a game based on Pac-Man. We worked on the character movement, game logic and other interactive elements required to make the game work.

This was one of the interesting activities of the week because I could see the output of the logic we created immediately. It also helped me understand basic programming concepts such as conditions, events and movements in a more practical way.

My Learning from Week 2

Week 2 gave me a combination of design thinking, logical thinking and programming experience. I learned how to think about a problem before solving it, how to break a problem into steps and how to turn those steps into a working application or game.

The main things I learned this week were:

  • Design Thinking
  • Logical problem-solving
  • Basic algorithms
  • Python fundamentals
  • MIT App Inventor
  • Mobile app development
  • Scratch game development
  • Teamwork and communication
