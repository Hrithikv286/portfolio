Week 4 — Prototyping, Selection, Fabrication & Discussion

Week 4 brought together digital 3D product design, physical fabrication, team formation, and critical public discussion. Building on the Fusion 360 skills developed in Week 3, this week focused on taking a design from the computer screen to a real physical object, while also developing teamwork, communication, and critical thinking through the selection and discussion activities.

Activity 1 — CAD / Fusion 360 Water Bottle Model

At the start of the week, I worked in Autodesk Fusion 360 to design a detailed 3D model of a water bottle. While building the model, I focused on the bottle's overall shape, proportions, and dimensions so that the design would be both realistic and functional. This activity helped me understand basic 3D modelling techniques and how product geometry is built up step by step, starting from simple sketches and developing into a complete solid body. I also learned how to review the model and prepare it for manufacturing, which gave me a clear understanding of the end-to-end workflow from digital design to 3D printing preparation.

Activity 2 — 3D Printing Activity

In a separate session, we explored the additive manufacturing process by 3D printing the water bottle model created in Fusion 360. The model was exported from Fusion 360 and prepared in slicer software, where we learned how settings such as layer height, infill, print speed, and supports affect the print quality, strength, and printing time of a part. Watching the printer build the model layer by layer helped me understand the principles of layer-by-layer fabrication and how decisions made during CAD modelling directly affect how easily a part can be printed. This activity showed me how a digital model is converted into a physical product.

Activity 3 — Visionary Selection & Alpha Team Formation

During the week, I participated in the Visionary selection process, which was conducted as part of the cohort evaluation. Following the selection process, I took part in the formal segregation and formation of Alpha teams, which were created to work on targeted innovation challenges. This activity helped me understand the importance of teamwork, how teams can be formed around members with complementary skills, and how to prepare for collaborative problem-solving in the upcoming challenges.

Activity 4 — Laser Cutting Fabrication

I also participated in a hands-on laser cutting activity as part of rapid prototyping and fabrication. We learned how a laser cutter follows vector paths from a digital design to cut and engrave materials precisely, and how the cutting speed and power need to be chosen according to the material being used. We also explored how flat laser-cut parts can be designed to fit together into a structural assembly. This activity helped me understand how laser cutting can be used to produce accurate parts quickly, making it a valuable method for building and testing prototypes.

Activity 5 — Social Media Debate / Group Discussion

We concluded the week on Saturday with a structured group discussion and debate on the topic “Social Media – Good Influencer or Bad?”. Arguments were presented on both sides, covering the benefits of social media, such as communication, learning, awareness, and opportunities for creators and businesses, as well as its drawbacks, such as misinformation, addiction, privacy concerns, and its effect on mental health. Taking part in the debate strengthened my communication and public speaking skills, improved my critical thinking, and taught me to listen to and evaluate diverse perspectives before forming my own opinion.

Overall, Week 4 was a valuable learning experience that connected digital design with physical fabrication. By modelling and 3D printing a water bottle and working with laser cutting, I gained practical experience in rapid prototyping and manufacturing processes. The Visionary selection and Alpha team formation prepared me for collaborative innovation challenges, while the group discussion improved my communication, critical thinking, and ability to consider different points of view.
