Protosem Immersion Program

Week 1 — 5S Activity and Portfolio Development

Zen Pencils — Procrastination Reflection

During the Zen Pencils activity, I related a comic to my own life. I chose the theme of procrastination, which encouraged me to reflect on how delaying tasks can affect progress and how taking immediate action can build better habits.

5S Activity — Battery Team

The first three days focused on a practical 5S activity: Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardize, and Sustain. As a member of the Battery Team, I helped organize different types of cables by type, size, and purpose, placing them in properly labelled storage boxes.

We verified labels, improved the arrangement of cables, and ensured the storage area remained clean, ordered, and easy to access. The activity strengthened my understanding of workplace organization, safety, teamwork, and attention to detail.

Portfolio Development

During the final two days, we began creating personal portfolios by organizing educational qualifications, technical skills, projects, achievements, certifications, and career goals into a professional format.
