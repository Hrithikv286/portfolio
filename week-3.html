Week 3 — Electronics Teardown & Autodesk Fusion 360 3D CAD Design

During the third week of my internship, I gained practical knowledge in electrical and electronic systems, 3D modelling, product design, and creative problem-solving. On Monday and Tuesday, we were introduced to the fundamentals of electrical and electronic systems. We learned about basic electrical components, their functions, circuit fundamentals, and how these components are used in practical applications. This helped me understand the basic principles required to work with electrical and electronic systems and provided a foundation for further learning in mechatronics and automation.

On Wednesday and Thursday, we were introduced to Autodesk Fusion 360 and learned about its various tools, features, and options used for 3D modelling and product design. We explored the basic modelling workflow and learned how different tools can be used to create and modify three-dimensional components. To apply this knowledge practically, I designed a tool kit using toy clay in the evening. This activity helped me understand the importance of shape, proportions, dimensions, and component arrangement before developing the design digitally. It also gave me a better understanding of how a physical concept can be converted into a 3D CAD model.

On Thursday evening, we participated in a paper rocket activity, where we designed and launched paper rockets as part of a competition to determine which rocket could travel the longest distance. The competition was conducted in two rounds. In the first round, we completed the activity without using a mobile phone, which encouraged us to depend on our own ideas and problem-solving abilities. In the second round, we were allowed to use a phone for assistance and reference. This activity helped us understand the importance of design, experimentation, teamwork, and improving a concept based on available information and results.

At the end of the week, we were given an assignment that combined the skills we had learned throughout the week. The assignment included modelling the clay tool kit and paper rocket using Fusion 360, along with designing a new component based on our own ideas. I worked on developing the models while focusing on the overall shape, dimensions, and design details. On Friday, we presented our completed designs and explained our modelling process during the evaluation. The feedback received during the evaluation helped me identify areas for improvement and understand how CAD modelling can be used to develop and communicate product ideas effectively. Overall, Week 3 was a valuable learning experience that improved my understanding of electrical and electronic fundamentals, Fusion 360, 3D modelling, product design, creativity, and practical problem-solving.
